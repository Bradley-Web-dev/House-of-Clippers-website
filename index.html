About

Barbering, Done
the Right Way

House of Clippers Barber Shop is a Houston barbershop serving the Gessner Road area with clean, precise haircuts and modern fades. The focus stays on the fundamentals — a sharp line, an even fade, and a finish that holds up long after you leave the chair.

Every visit gets the same attention to detail, whether it's a classic cut, a full beard grooming session, or a fresh style before a big week. It's a straightforward, professional space built for anyone who wants to look sharp without the fuss.
